Privacy policy

SolarRT — website, Windows app and Android app · Effective 17 November 2026

Summary

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no cookies.
  • Your chosen location is saved only on your device.
  • To show your forecast, your location's coordinates are sent to the SolarRT server and passed on to weather and place-name services. They are not stored.
  • Feedback is stored only if you choose to send it.

Who is responsible

SolarRT is developed and operated by S. Debnath ("we"). Contact us through the feedback form in the app. If you'd like a reply, include your contact details.

What we process and why

Your location

SolarRT shows aurora chances, weather and local time for a location you choose. It can come from your device's location (only with your permission), from your IP address (see below), from a place you search for, or from a point you pick on the map.

  • On your device: the chosen location, theme, time-zone choice and alert settings are kept in your browser's or app's local storage. They never leave your device as stored data. Clear them by clearing the site's or app's data.
  • Sent to the SolarRT server: the coordinates, with each request for your forecast, weather or place name. The server uses them to answer the request and keeps short-lived copies of the answers (for example, the weather for a 1 km grid square) in memory for up to 24 hours so it can reply faster. Request logs that would contain coordinates are switched off.

Approximate location from your IP address

Only if you choose "Use approximate location", your browser or app asks geojs.io (or, as a fallback, ipapi.co) to estimate your town from your IP address. That request goes directly from your device to that service.

IP address (technical)

Like any website, the server sees your IP address when you connect. It's used in memory only to protect the service from abuse (rate limiting) and is not written to logs or stored.

Feedback

If you send feedback, we store your rating, your comment, any contact details you choose to give, the app edition and version, and the time. It's used only to improve SolarRT and to reply to you. It's kept for up to 24 months, or deleted sooner on request. Please don't include sensitive information in comments.

Notifications

Aurora alerts are generated on your device from the forecast data. No push service or notification ID is involved.

Services we pass requests to

  • Open-Meteo (weather): coordinates, rounded to about 1 km.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (place search and names): your search text or coordinates.
  • NOAA SWPC, CelesTrak and EUMETSAT (aurora, ISS and cloud data): no personal data.
  • Esri (map images) and EUMETSAT (cloud images) are loaded directly by your browser or app, so these services see your IP address and the map area you view, as with any online map.

We do not sell or share personal data for any other purpose.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK/EU GDPR), you can ask to access, correct or delete feedback you sent, or object to its processing. Contact us through the feedback form. The legal basis for processing feedback is your consent, and for answering forecast requests it is our legitimate interest in providing the service you ask for.

Children

SolarRT is a general-audience science app and doesn't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS, the server is hardened and rate-limited, and only the operator can read feedback.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
